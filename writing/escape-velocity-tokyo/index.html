Why SATELYX is starting Escape Velocity in Tokyo

25 November 2026 · SATELYX

On Wednesday 2 December, SATELYX presents Escape Velocity: Nihonbashi Edition, the first evening of a new event series we are starting for the people who fund, launch and use space technology. IVS and Transcelestial are co-hosting it with us, and I am one of the event's hosts.

Three conversations in one room

Most of my work at SATELYX is commercial: talking to the developers who want to fly with us and to the partners around them. The developers are usually working on three things at once. They need money to build hardware that has not flown yet, a flight that fits their schedule, and a way to turn results from orbit into something a customer will buy. Each of those conversations tends to happen in a different room, at investor events, launch conferences or technical meetings.

That is also the problem our missions are built around. SATELYX builds and operates its own satellites for shared in-orbit demonstration (IOD) missions, and the teams we fly need access to orbit, evidence that customers and investors will accept, and people who can help put the technology to use once it has flown. Escape Velocity puts the people behind each of those in one room for an evening, so they can hear how the others think and meet people they could work with.

Why Tokyo first

The evening is an official side event of NIHONBASHI SPACE WEEK, which runs from 1 to 4 December, organised by cross U and co-organised by Mitsui Fudosan. SATELYX is a member of cross U, part of our team is based in Japan, and many of the founders, investors and launch teams we want to meet will be in Nihonbashi that week.

What the evening covers

The theme is Building the Next Space Backbone: Capital, Access to Orbit, and Networks for Intelligence. Three panels cover it:

  • Capital, with investors on where private money in space is going and what convinces them to back a company.
  • Access to orbit, with speakers from across the launch business on availability, cadence and keeping launch capacity in step with demand.
  • Networks for intelligence, led by Transcelestial, on how US and Japanese companies are building resilient space communications.

After the third panel the room stays open for networking, so there is time to follow up with the speakers and meet other guests. The SATELYX announcement names every moderator and speaker.

Details

  • Wednesday 2 December 2026, 18:30 to 21:30 JST.
  • xBridge Yaesu, Tokyo, a three-minute walk from the Yaesu North Exit of Tokyo Station.
  • Presented by SATELYX, co-hosted by IVS and Transcelestial.
  • Attendance is free, and you request a place on Luma.

After Tokyo

Tokyo is the first stop. We plan to take Escape Velocity to other cities and use it to connect space communities across countries, including the UK, Japan, Taiwan and Portugal, because a founder in one country often needs investors, launch providers or customers in another. We will announce the next cities once plans are confirmed.

If you are in Tokyo that week, request a place and join us.
